Sign Out of Your Current Apple ID

Sign out of your Apple ID in the App Store.

Instructions

Open the App Store → tap your profile picture in the upper-right corner → scroll to the bottom → tap Sign Out.

Important: On devices running iOS 26 or later, go to Settings → Apple Account at the top → Media & Purchases → Sign Out. When signing in with a shared ID, choose Not ***. When signing back in with your own ID, you may tap Continue.

2
Sign In With a Shared Apple ID

Sign in to the App Store with the shared Apple ID we provide, then search for and download digilink.

Please read the safety warnings before continuing

1. Apps cannot be updated with a shared Apple ID. To update an app, uninstall it and reinstall it; otherwise, you may see an account-disabled message.

2. A shared Apple ID may be used only in the App Store (or Media & Purchases). Never sign in to iCloud in Settings, or your device may become locked.

3. If a two-factor authentication prompt appears, select Other Options, then Don't Upgrade. Do not tap Continue or link your phone number.

4. If sign-in requires an SMS verification code, someone has linked a phone number to the account. Use another ID or wait for the ID to be repaired automatically.

5. Shared IDs are automatically repaired once per hour. If neither ID works, wait for the repair and request an ID again.
